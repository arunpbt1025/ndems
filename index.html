      Standard Operating Procedures & Statutory Guidelines

      Official forensic protocols for state law enforcement & judicial agencies.

      Key Statutory Standards:

      • Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (Section 63): Mandates that any electronic record must carry an immutable cryptographic hash and custodian declaration before court admission.

      • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: Mandates strict discovery and access restriction for citizen identifiers present in surveillance and messaging records.

      • RFC 6234 (SHA-256 Digest Standard): Prescribes collision-resistant 256-bit hashing as the foundational mathematical fingerprint.
